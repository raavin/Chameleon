# Domain XIII: Disability, Neurodiversity & Accessibility — The Sovereignty & Inclusion Manifest

In the Chameleon Protocol, **Domain XIII** is the "Self-Determination Engine." It moves beyond the "Medical Model" of disability—which views people as problems to be fixed—to the **Social and Human Rights Models**, where the focus is on removing societal barriers and empowering individual agency. It treats accessibility not as a feature, but as a non-negotiable prerequisite for all other 12 domains.

This deep-dive provides the AI Factory with the architectural DNA to govern **Individualized Funding (NDIS-style)**, **Neuro-affirming clinical pathways**, **Universal Design for Public Infrastructure**, and **Sovereign Assistive Technology**.

---

## 1. The Inclusion Taxonomy: Global Service Archetypes

The system must identify the "Agency Logic" to apply the correct rights-based frameworks and funding guardrails.

### A. Individualized Funding & Self-Management

* **NDIS-Style Personalized Budgets:** Direct control of funding for the person with a disability to purchase their own supports.
* **Support Coordination:** Managing the links between formal services and informal community networks.
* **Plan Management:** Financial oversight and automated acquittal of service invoices (Domain VII linkage).

### B. Neuro-Affirming Care & Support

* **Autism & ADHD Support:** Services that validate neuro-differences rather than enforcing "normative" behavior.
* **Sensory-Aware Environments:** Managing physical spaces via the Hub to reduce sensory overload (e.g., smart-lighting and noise-cancellation logic).
* **Communication Access (AAC):** Integrating Augmentative and Alternative Communication tools directly into the "Spoke" interface.

### C. Physical Accessibility & Universal Design

* **Built Environment Auditing:** Digital twin mapping of ramps, lifts, and tactile paths in public infrastructure (Domain IX linkage).
* **Transport Accessibility:** Real-time logging of wheelchair-accessible fleet availability (Domain VIII linkage).
* **Digital Inclusion:** Hard-coded compliance with **WCAG 2.1/2.2 AAA** standards for all Chameleon interfaces.

### D. Rights Monitoring & Advocacy

* **UNCRPD Compliance:** Automated reporting against the **UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities**.
* **Restrictive Practice Oversight:** Mandatory "Two-Key" sign-off and WORM logging for any limitation of liberty, with immediate alerts to an independent Advocate (Domain VI linkage).
* **Sovereign Assistive Tech:** Tracking the lifecycle of wheelchairs, prosthetics, and communication devices, including "Right to Repair" documentation.

## 3. UX Stories: The Experience of Agency

### Story 1: The Self-Manager (Financial Sovereignty)

**The Scenario:** A wheelchair user in Melbourne is hiring a new support worker.
**The UX:** The user opens the `DIS_PLAN_BUDGET` on their phone.
**The Action:** They review the worker’s "Verified Competency Hash" (Domain 8). They "Accept" the worker into their plan.
**The Governance:** Payment is only released from the user’s digital wallet when the user (or their nominee) taps to confirm the shift was completed. This eliminates **"Provider Capture"** where agencies control the money.

### Story 2: The Non-Speaking Student (Communication Access)

**The Scenario:** A child who uses a symbol-board is attending a rural school (Domain 9).
**The UX:** The teacher’s tablet (Spoke) automatically switches to "AAC Sync Mode."
**The Action:** The child uses their own device to select "I need a break." The teacher's device receives the alert instantly.
**The Governance:** The `DIS_AAC_SPEAK` log records that the student’s communication right was respected, providing evidence for the annual `UNCRPD` audit.

### Story 3: The Neurodivergent Professional (Environment Control)

**The Scenario:** An employee with sensory sensitivities is working in a communal NGO office.
**The UX:** As they walk into a room, the Hub recognizes their `DIS_SENSORY_MAP`.
**The Action:** The smart-lights dim to 30%, and the white-noise machine activates.


**The Experience:** The software "bends to the will" of the user's nervous system, reducing the cognitive load of navigating a hostile environment.

## 4. The Panopticon of Integrity: Ending "Disability Fraud" & Abuse

The disability sector is high-risk for financial exploitation by providers and physical abuse by staff.

1. **The "Nominee-Check" Agent:** If a guardian or nominee is managing a client’s budget, the system triggers randomized `GOV_PHOTO_VERIFY` checks to ensure the funds are actually being spent on the client’s needs (e.g., equipment or social outings).
2. **Anti-Restrictive Practice Shield:** Any use of "Chemical or Physical Restraint" requires a `GOV_TWO_KEY` authorization from an external clinical lead. The system generates an immutable WORM record that cannot be hidden from the Senior Practitioner or Public Advocate.


3. **Provider "Rate-Cap" Enforcer:** The AI "Sentinel" (Domain VII) automatically blocks any invoice that exceeds the legislated price-guide, preventing "Disability Price-Gouging".

## 5. Global Adaptability & Sovereignty

* **Developed Context:** Focuses on integration with **National Disability Insurance Schemes**, **Employment Participation** (Domain 8), and **Web Accessibility Law**.
* **Developing Context:** Focuses on **Community-Based Rehabilitation (CBR)**. The system uses the **CARE Principles** to empower local "Disability Committees" to manage collective resources (e.g., a shared van or prosthetic lab).


* **Crisis/Displaced Context:** The protocol mandates a "Disability-First Triage" in Domain V. Refugee camps must use the `DIS_ACCESS_CHECK` tool to ensure that latrines and aid-distribution points are physically reachable.
