# Domain II: Mental Health & Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) — The Sanctuary Manifest

In the Chameleon Protocol, **Domain II** is treated as a "Sanctuary Space." Unlike the physical interventions of Domain I, Mental Health governance must manage the **intangible**: trauma, safety, and the absolute sanctity of the therapeutic alliance.

This deep dive establishes the master logic for the AI Factory to generate systems that are legally bulletproof, clinically rigorous, and deeply empathetic. We are building a system that understands that in mental health, **data is not just information—it is a potential weapon or a tool for healing.**

---

## 1. The MHPSS Taxonomy: The Breadth of Care

The AI must identify the specific "Environmental Logic" of the program. Mental health care varies wildly from involuntary inpatient wards to community-based trauma healing in war zones.

### A. Specialized & Acute Care

* **Inpatient Psychiatric Units:** Governance of "Involuntary Detention" clocks (The Mental Health Act), seclusion/restraint logs (statutory reporting), and medication titration.
* **Crisis Assessment & Treatment (CATS):** High-intensity community outreach, suicide intervention, and "Police-Ambulance" co-response workflows.
* **Neuropsychology & Specialized Assessment:** Complex testing for dementia, TBI (Traumatic Brain Injury), and neurodivergence.

### B. Community & Therapeutic Services

* **Counseling & Psychotherapy:** Longitudinal care for depression, anxiety, and PTSD.
* **Youth Early Intervention:** Specialized logic for "Headspace" style models, focusing on youth-friendly UX and school-integrated care.
* **AOD-MH Dual Diagnosis:** The "No Wrong Door" logic where Domain II and Domain III (AOD) logic merges into a single integrated care plan.

### C. Humanitarian & Refugee MHPSS (IASC Standard)

* **Psychological First Aid (PFA):** Rapid triage in disaster zones.
* **Community Healing Circles:** Group-based logic where a "Single Case" is a communal entity (Kinship mapping).
* **Trauma-Informed Displacement Care:** Tracking the mental impact of migration and statelessness.

## 2. The "Privacy Barrier" Architecture

In Domain II, the **Panopticon** (transparency) must be balanced with the **Sanctuary** (secrecy).

### The Logic of Information Barriers

A world-class MH system uses **Zero-Knowledge Encryption** for case notes.

* **The Rule:** The "Metadata" (attendance, dates, medication) is visible to the organization for billing and safety. The "Content" (therapy notes) is encrypted with a key held only by the clinician and the client.
* **The Overload (Section 144 / Duty of Care):** The system includes a `PRIV_BREAK_GLASS` component. If a clinician marks a "High Risk of Harm to Others," the encryption is bypassed *only* for specific safety-critical fields to allow for police or child protection intervention.

## 4. UX Stories: The MHPSS Experience

### Story 1: The "High-Risk" Therapist (Melbourne, Australia)

**The Scenario:** A therapist is seeing a client who reveals they have a plan to harm themselves tonight.
**The UX:** The therapist clicks the `MH_RISK_MATRIX`. As they enter the "Plan" and "Access to Means," the UI changes. A persistent **Red Banner** appears: *"Safety Plan Required."* **The Action:** The system prevents the therapist from "Closing the Session" until a Safety Plan is generated and a "Check-in" task is assigned to the Morning Shift. This is the "Software as a Colleague" bending to the will of clinical safety.

### Story 2: The Community Worker (Dadaab Refugee Camp, Kenya)

**The Scenario:** A worker is conducting a "Healing Circle" for 10 women.
**The UX:** The worker uses a tablet with the `ID_KIN_MAP` component. They don't see 10 individual clinical files; they see a communal view of the "Circle."
**The Action:** They record a single "Communal Intervention." The AI Factory handles the back-end distribution of that note into 10 separate, anonymized records to satisfy WHO reporting standards without duplicating work.

## 5. The Integrity & Ethics Layer: Preventing Abuse

In mental health, power imbalances can lead to abuse. The Chameleon Protocol bakes "Patient Rights" into the code.

1. **The "Rights Advocate" Flag:** If a patient is placed on an Involuntary Order, the system automatically generates a "Notification Task" for a local Independent Mental Health Advocate.
2. **Seclusion/Restraint Panopticon:** These events are high-risk for human rights violations. Any entry into a "Seclusion Log" triggers an immediate **High-Level Governance Alert** on the Regional Auditor’s dashboard, requiring a `GOV_TWO_KEY` review of the justification within 2 hours.
3. **Audit of Access:** The client can request a "Transparency Report" showing exactly who has viewed their record and why. This builds the trust necessary for therapeutic success.
