## 1. The Family Taxonomy: Global Service Archetypes

The system must identify the "Systemic Logic" to apply the correct protective filters, support tiers, and cultural kinship rules.

### A. Family Preservation & Early Intervention

* **Family Strengthening Programs:** Preventative support for families at risk of breakdown due to poverty (Domain V), substance use (Domain III), or mental health (Domain II).
* **Home Visiting & Early Childhood Support:** Nurse-led and peer-led interventions during the "First 1000 Days" of a child's life.
* **Parenting Education & Coaching:** Evidence-based programs (e.g., Triple P, Circle of Security) with integrated "Milestone Tracking."

### B. Family Welfare & Alternative Care

* **Family Reunification:** Intensive support and legal monitoring (Domain VI) to return children from state care to their biological families.
* **Kinship & Foster Care Support:** Managing the financial (Domain VII) and emotional support for non-biological caregivers.
* **Adoption & Permanent Care:** Secure, longitudinal tracking of identity and medical history (Domain I) for adopted persons.

### C. Domestic & Family Stability

* **Family Mediation & Dispute Resolution:** Neutral, data-driven frameworks for managing separation, custody, and property without high-conflict litigation.
* 
**Domestic Violence Recovery:** Integrated support for the whole family unit, including specialized "Safety Plans" for children (Domain VI linkage).


* **Economic Family Resilience:** Managing "Family Budgets," child support payments, and household-level social protection grants (Domain VII linkage).

### D. Kinship & Communal Structures (The Global Model)

* 
**Indigenous Kinship Systems:** Mapping complex family trees that define care responsibilities, land rights (Domain IV), and cultural identity (Domain XIV).


* **Multi-generational Households:** Services designed for the "Sandwich Generation"—those caring for both children (Domain VIII) and elderly parents (Domain XII).
