## 4. The Panopticon of Integrity: Ending "Sporting Abuse"

The sport and youth sector is high-risk for grooming, physical abuse, and the misappropriation of "Development Funds."

1. **The Anti-Grooming Shield:** The system monitors "Private Communication" flags. If a staff member attempts to message a youth member outside of the "Moderated Hub," the system triggers a mandatory review.
2. **Ghost-Participant Audit:** To acquit "Sport for Development" grants, the system requires `EDU_ATTEND_BIO` (biometric) roll-calls. This prevents NGOs from claiming they are serving 500 children when only 50 are present.
3. **Integrity-First Scouting:** The `SPT_INT_WORM` prevents "Age Fraud" in youth sports—a common issue in international scouting—by anchoring birth records to the Domain I (Health) and Domain XIV (Cultural/Elder) verification chains.
