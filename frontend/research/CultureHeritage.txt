## 4. The Panopticon of Integrity: Ending "Cultural Extraction"

Indigenous knowledge has historically been extracted for research or profit without consent.

1. **Anti-Extraction Shield:** Any research data exported from the system must pass through a "Sovereignty Filter." The system automatically blocks the export of any record tagged with `CUL_GUARD_RAIL` unless a community-led "Two-Key" authorization is provided.
2. **Ancestral Audit Trail:** Every time a non-community member (e.g., a government auditor) views a cultural record, the system notifies the community's "Digital Guardian" in real-time.
3. **Immutable Provenance:** For art and medicine, the `CUL_IP_LEDGER` ensures a permanent, unchangeable link between the knowledge and the original community, preventing "Bio-Piracy."
