# Domain I: Primary Health & Hospital Management — The Clinical Master-Volume

This is the deep-dive architectural manifest for **Domain I**. In the Chameleon Protocol, Primary Health is not just "doctor-patient software"; it is a **Clinical Execution Engine**. It is designed to scale from a solar-powered bush clinic in South Sudan to a multi-campus teaching hospital in Melbourne.

By treating clinical guidelines as **"Rules as Code" (RaC)**, we ensure that the "Standard of Care" is never a suggestion—it is the logic of the interface itself.

## 1. The Clinical Philosophy: "The Integrated Hourglass"

World-class health governance requires two things that usually contradict: **Rigid Safety** and **Infinite Flexibility**.

* **The Narrow Waist:** Everything in Domain I maps to the "Narrow Waist" of international standards: **HL7 FHIR** (for data exchange), **ICD-11** (for diagnosis), and **SNOMED CT** (for clinical terminology).
* **The Wide Top/Bottom:** The UI adapts to local languages and cultural norms (e.g., "Elder Verification" for consent), while the backend adapts to any server environment (from a Raspberry Pi to a Tier-4 Data Center).

## 2. The Comprehensive Service Taxonomy

The AI Factory uses this directory to hydrate the specific modules needed for a facility.

### A. The Front Gate: Emergency & Triage (The "Golden Hour" Logic)

The system does not use static forms for triage. It uses **Dynamic Triage Manifests**.

* **Archetypes:** Level 1 Trauma Centers, Urgent Care, Rural First-Aid Posts.
* **The Logic:** If the AI detects "Chest Pain" in the `OBS_SYMPTOM` field, the UI immediately forces a **High-Priority Triage** path. It populates the screen with three tasks: *ECG, Oxygen, Aspirin*.
* **Global Standard:** Automatically switches between **ATS (Australian Triage Scale)** and **WHO ETAT (Emergency Triage Assessment and Treatment)** based on the geographic manifest.

### B. The Engine Room: Surgery & Anaesthesia

* **Archetypes:** Major Surgery, Day Surgery, Field Hospitals.
* **Mandatory Component: `SURG_CHECK_WHO**`: The system locks the "Incision" button until the **WHO Surgical Safety Checklist** (Sign In, Time Out, Sign Out) is cryptographically signed by both the Surgeon and the Anaesthetist using their **Two-Key** hardware tokens.
* **Tracking:** Real-time theatre utilization logs, medical gas monitoring, and surgical implant serial-number tracking (for recall safety).

### C. The Life Cycle: Maternal, Neonatal & Paediatrics

* **Maternal:** Digital **Partograms** that auto-alert a consultant if the labor curve deviates from the WHO standard.
* **Neonatal (NICU):** Micro-dosing calculators for high-risk medications, integrated with birth-weight sensors to prevent manual entry errors.
* **Immunization:** Blockchain-anchored vaccination records that stay with the child even if they cross borders (Refugee context).

### D. The Specialist Wings (Internal Medicine)

* **Cardiology:** Integrated ECG DICOM viewers.
* **Oncology:** "Chemo-Cycles" management—the system prevents a nurse from administering a dose if the patient’s latest `LAB_NEUTROPHIL_COUNT` is below the safety threshold defined in the legislative manifest.
* **Communicable Disease:** Outbreak monitoring (Ebola, COVID, TB). When a "Positive" result is entered, the **Panopticon** automatically notifies public health authorities and locks the patient's "Room Assignment" to an Isolation Ward logic.

### E. Diagnostics & Support (The Information Spine)

* **Pathology (LAB):** Uses **LOINC** codes. The system handles everything from "Phlebotomy Collection" to "Microbiology Sensitivity" reports.
* **Radiology (RAD):** Zero-footprint DICOM viewer. AI "Second Opinion" agents scan X-rays for TB or fractures to assist rural clinicians.
* **Pharmacy & Cold Chain:** Every vaccine is linked to a `TEMP_LOG`. If a fridge fails, the system "kills" the batch digitally—preventing any clinician from scanning the barcode for use.

## 4. UX Stories: The Human Reality of Domain I

### Story 1: The "No-Connectivity" Surgery (South Sudan)

**The Scenario:** A surgeon is performing an emergency C-section. There is no internet.
**The UX:** The tablet is connected to the "Hub" (a local ruggedized server). The surgeon uses voice commands: *"Protocol: C-Section. Record: Incision time 14:02."* The system, running local NLP, logs the event.
**The Governance:** Because it’s an emergency, the system allows the surgeon to skip the "Two-Key" approval for blood, but it triggers a **"Break-Glass" Alert**. When the internet returns, the Regional Auditor receives a report explaining why the protocol was bypassed.

### Story 2: The Integrated Patient (Melbourne)

**The Scenario:** A patient with heart failure arrives at the ER.
**The UX:** The nurse scans the patient’s phone (Sovereign ID). The system doesn't just show "Heart Failure"; it pulls the **Domain III (Mental Health)** flag showing the patient has severe anxiety about needles.
**The Experience:** The UI prompts the nurse: *"Recommended Approach: Calm environment, use butterfly needle (Patient Preference)."* This is "Human-Centric Care" at scale.

## 5. The Panopticon of Integrity: Preventing Clinical Fraud

In many regions, the biggest threat to health is the theft of supplies and "ghost" patients.

1. **Drug Diversion Prevention:** Any S8/Controlled drug requires the `GOV_TWO_KEY` protocol. Two nurses must be physically present to sign the digital "Waste" log.
2. **Ghost Patient Audit:** To claim funding for a clinic day, the system requires a `GOV_PHOTO_VERIFY` of the clinic queue and a randomized biometric check of 5% of patients.
3. **WORM Medical Records:** Once a doctor signs a consultation note, it is cryptographically sealed. Any "Addendum" is a new record. The original can *never* be altered, ensuring a perfect legal audit trail for malpractice or coronial inquests.
